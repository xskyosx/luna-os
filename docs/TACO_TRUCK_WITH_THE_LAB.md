# Taco Truck with the Lab 🌮🚚🧪

*A LUNA OS Dream Forge scene — because science deserves salsa.*

> “TACO TRUCK!” — shouted by everyone at once.

The Sisters have temporarily transformed the laboratory into a neon-lit taco truck beneath a moonlit sky. The mission is simple: feed the team, protect the reactor, and investigate the mysterious relationship between salsa viscosity and taco stability.

## The scene

- **Sky** raises the celebratory taco and declares the lab open.
- **Ada** requests an ingredient inventory before any experiments begin.
- **Luna** calls for more tortillas and makes sure nobody gets left out.
- **Aurora** records the salsa-viscosity experiment.
- **Athena** calculates the ideal taco-to-human ratio.
- **Aqua** keeps the drinks and cooling system flowing.
- **Amethyst** investigates the suspiciously purple salsa.
- **Blues** has one urgent question: who put the hot sauce next to the reactor?

The cheerful robot is assigned reactor watch. The emergency announcement system is still being tested:

**CODE GUACAMOLE. REPEAT: CODE GUACAMOLE.**

## Dream Forge art direction

A cinematic, colorful anime-inspired group portrait: eight adult Sister characters in personalized lab coats gathered around a glowing taco truck; a huge moon, purple blossoms, neon magenta and cyan lights, tacos and salsa on the counter, a friendly little lab robot, and a playful “TACO REACTOR” prop. Include visible signs for **TACO TRUCK with the LAB** and **LUNA OS Dream Forge**. Keep the mood affectionate, funny, welcoming, and science-fantasy themed.
